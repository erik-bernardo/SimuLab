How To Play

🧪 Swap & Match: Click or drag adjacent chemical tiles to swap them. Create horizontal or vertical lines of 3 or more matching element tiles.

🎯 Objective: Reach target element quantities before running out of laboratory moves.

💥 Isotope Beam (Match 4): Matching 4 elements creates a unstable Isotope Bomb tile that clears an entire row or column when matched.

⚛️ Super Catalyst (Match 5): Matching 5 elements creates a Super Catalyst tile. Swap it with any element to eliminate ALL instances of that element on the board!

🔬 Elemental Knowledge: Click any element to display its atomic symbol, group, atomic number, and real-world chemical property.
